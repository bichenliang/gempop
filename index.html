Jewelry Care Guide

1. Keep jewelry away from perfume, lotion, soap, seawater and pool water; chemicals speed up rhodium plating fading.

2. Remove jewelry before showering, exercise or housework; sweat accelerates coating wear.

3. Store in sealed dry plastic bags when not worn, avoid damp bathroom storage.

4. Minor plating fading after long daily wear is natural wear and tear, not product quality defect.
