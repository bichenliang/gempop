Jewelry Care Tips

1. Keep away from perfume, lotion, soap, seawater and pool water; chemicals speed up rhodium plating fading.

2. Remove jewelry before showering, exercise or housework; sweat accelerates coating wear.

3. Store in sealed dry plastic bags when not worn, avoid damp bathroom storage.

4. Avoid hard collision and squeezing stone prongs to prevent gemstone loosening.

Important Note: Minor plating fading after long daily wear is natural wear and tear, not product quality defect.

Petua Penjagaan Barang Kemas

1. Jauhkan daripada minyak wangi, losyen, sabun, air laut & air kolam; bahan kimia cepatkan salutan rodium luntur.

2. Tanggalkan barang kemas sebelum mandi, bersukan atau kerja rumah; peluh hauskan salutan dengan cepat.

3. Simpan di beg plastik kedap udara kering apabila tidak dipakai, elak simpan di bilik mandi lembap.

4. Elak hentakan keras & tekan cakar batu untuk elak batu permata longgar.

Nota Penting: Sedikit luntur salutan selepas dipakai lama setiap hari adalah pemakaian semulajadi biasa, bukan kecacatan produk.
